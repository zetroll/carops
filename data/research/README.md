# Raw Research Dumps

Per-incubator research files. One markdown per incubator. Source: web-research agents run on 2026-04-29 against the Top 30 list provided in the project brief.

Layout:
- `tier-a/` — high probability for ThoughtKraft (10 incubators)
- `tier-b/` — strong but competitive (10)
- `tier-c/` — elite, hardest (10)

**Each file is a research artifact, not a strategy doc.** Strategy / IC-pitch / outreach guidance lives in `reports/incubators/` (synthesized in Phase 2).

## Known limitations of the captured data

- Most official incubator sites returned HTTP 403 to automated WebFetch — names compiled from search snippets, ZaubaCorp, Crunchbase, TheOrg, news, LinkedIn.
- Many "next deadline" fields say "rolling — next IC cycle" because programs don't publish forward-looking deadlines.
- Selection-committee names are public-data only. Internal IC composition for any given cycle is rarely published.
- One user-supplied premise did not verify: "Axilor is now part of Accel" — Axilor remains independent as of 2026.

## Corrections vs. original brief that show up in this data

- **SRiX** = SRIIC at SRIHER, **healthcare-only** — not a general Tier-A program. (Top-30 ranked it 5th by probability; sector mismatch makes it SKIP for ThoughtKraft.)
- **CIIE.CO** rebranded to **IIMA Ventures** (2024).
- **MUTBI** is gated to MAHE alumni — *flips to viable* given founder is MIT-Manipal alum.
- **i-Hub Gujarat Srujan** is age-gated (≤35) AND Gujarat-affiliation gated. Other i-Hub programs may be open.
- **NASSCOM Emerge 50** is the most actionable near-term call (closes ~17 May 2026).

## Re-running

Quarterly, or before any major application push. Web-fetch limitations mean a manual verification call/email to each program is a good complement.
