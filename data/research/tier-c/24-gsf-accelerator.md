# GSF Accelerator

- **URL**: https://www.gsfindia.com/
- **Apply**: https://www.gsfindia.com/founders-academy
- **Intake model**: Cohort — Founders Academy is 10–12 weeks/90-day program; reviewed on rolling basis with periodic cohort starts.
- **Next deadline (60-90d)**: applications open and being reviewed as of April 2026 — no fixed public deadline; rolling intake.
- **Reapply policy**: not surfaced.
- **Selection committee / board (public)**:
  - Rajesh Sawhney — Founder & CEO, GSF; ex-Reliance Entertainment
  - Alok Goyal — Stellaris (VC Mentor)
  - Chinmaya Saxena — VC Mentor
  - Pratik Poddar — Nexus Venture Partners (VC Mentor)
  - Rajan Anandan — Peak XV (VC Mentor)
  - Backed by 20 digital founders + 5 VC funds (names not individually surfaced)
- **Stated criteria**: founding team strength, market opportunity, traction, sector fit (SaaS/Enterprise/AI focus).
- **Funding/equity**: Founders Academy is NOT an accelerator with equity — explicitly "no equity stake, no T&Cs". Separate GSF SuperAngels invest equity.
- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: not surfaced (private fund/program).
- **Sector/stage filter**: AI-first, SaaS, enterprise; early-stage founding teams in India + SE Asia.
- **Red flags**: lower-touch program (mostly mentoring sessions); not a capital provider for Academy track — for capital, must convert via SuperAngels separately.
- **ThoughtKraft fit**: MEDIUM — Academy is no-equity (matches grants-only ask) but no direct cash either. Value = network access + mentor connection (Rajesh Sawhney's reach). Father (ex-VP Reliance Industries) and Sawhney (ex-Reliance Entertainment) both Reliance-adjacent — possible warm-intro vector.
