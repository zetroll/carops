# NSRCEL (IIM Bangalore)

- **URL**: https://nsrcel.org/
- **Apply**: https://nsrcel.org/launchpad/ + program-specific pages
- **Intake model**: Cohort-based, multiple program tracks (Launchpad, Healthcare, Mobility, Women Startup, Goldman Sachs 10K Women, Revenue-stage).
- **Next deadline (60-90d)**: Launchpad ~05 Apr 2026 (window may have closed — verify); Healthcare Incubation ~26 Apr 2026 (likely closed by 2026-04-29). Watch for next Launchpad cycle Q3 2026.
- **Reapply policy**: not surfaced.
- **Selection committee / board (public)**:
  - Anand Sri Ganesh — CEO, NSRCEL / IIMB Innovations
  - Prof. Srivardhini K Jha — Chairperson, NSRCEL / Faculty Entrepreneurship, IIMB
  - Director, IIM Bangalore — Chairperson, IIMB Innovations (Sec 8 parent)
  - 50+ panel of mentors (entrepreneurs, IIMB alumni, investors); 120+ broader mentor pool — names not individually published
- **Stated criteria**: founder mindset, idea/MVP stage fit, sector relevance, scalability; varies by program track.
- **Funding/equity**: Launchpad — top 3 receive grants up to ₹5L, no fee, no equity for Launchpad. Other tracks may channel SISFS (₹20L grant / ₹50L convertible).
- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: DPIIT-recognized incubator; channels SISFS.
- **Sector/stage filter**: idea + MVP + revenue-stage; SaaS/AI/ML accepted; post-revenue OK via Revenue-Stage Entrepreneurship Program.
- **Red flags**: multiple parallel programs — easy to misroute application; competitive (silver jubilee 2025).
- **ThoughtKraft fit**: STRONG for Revenue-Stage Entrepreneurship Program (RSEP) — ₹1L MRR matches profile. Bangalore physical preferred. Watch site for next RSEP cohort opening.
