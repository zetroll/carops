# IIT Madras Incubation Cell (IITMIC)

- **URL**: http://rtbi.in/incubationiitm/ ; https://www.iitm.ac.in/research-park/incubation-cell
- **Apply**: https://rtbi.in/incubationiitm/incubation/apply.html
- **Intake model**: Rolling applications, periodic IC review cycles. 500+ startups incubated; ~55% external founders.
- **Next deadline (60-90d)**: rolling — next IC cycle (no fixed public deadline).
- **Reapply policy**: not surfaced.
- **Selection committee / board (public)**:
  - Dr. Tamaswati Ghosh — CEO, IITMIC (since 2013); also heads RTBI + Bioincubator
  - Prof. V. Kamakoti — Chairman, IITMIC Board; Director, IIT Madras
  - IITMIC Board of Directors at http://rtbi.in/incubationiitm/about-us/management.html (not retrievable)
- **Stated criteria**: deep-tech/IP focus, scientific innovation, founder strength, scalability; IIT-M alignment preferred but not mandatory.
- **Funding/equity**: equity-free grants via NIDHI-PRAYAS (~₹10L), NIDHI-SSS (up to ~₹25-50L); SISFS host; equity investment via IITM RuTAG/Pravartak/IPSF for select startups.
- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: DPIIT-recognized; DST NIDHI host; SISFS host; flagship deep-tech incubator.
- **Sector/stage filter**: deep-tech focus (hardware, biotech, mobility, semiconductor, AI, climate). MSME commerce-OS borderline (more applied SaaS than deep-tech) — pitch the AI infra angle.
- **Red flags**: strong deep-tech bias; pure SaaS may face "fit" challenges; competitive (500+ portfolio, 2 unicorns, Ather IPO).
- **ThoughtKraft fit**: MEDIUM — external founders accepted ✓; equity-free grants ✓; sector fit needs reframing as "AI infrastructure". Apply with deep-tech IP narrative (which 7 modules are IP-defensible?).
