# CIE IIIT Hyderabad (Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship)

- **URL**: https://cie.iiit.ac.in/
- **Apply**: https://cie.iiit.ac.in/accelerator-programs/ (Avishkar online form)
- **Intake model**: Cohort — Avishkar accelerator runs 2 cohorts/year (Summer + Winter), 6 months each. 17+ cohorts since 2016.
- **Next deadline (60-90d)**: Summer cohort window typically opens Q2; specific 2026 dates not surfaced — apply ASAP via portal.
- **Reapply policy**: not surfaced.
- **Selection committee / board (public)**:
  - C.V. Jawahar — CEO, CIE IIITH
  - Prof. Ramesh Loganathan — COO
  - Resident Mentors: Prof. Anoop Namboodri, Prof. Bapi Raju, Prof. Sachin Chaudhari, Prof. Manish Shrivastava (IIIT-H faculty)
  - Operational team: Avanija Ramanan, Nirupa Vijaykumar, Erin Miller, Pankaj Dwivedi, Neha Darshanam, Rajat Somvanshi
- **Stated criteria**: team, tech (AI/ML/CV/NLP/DeepTech depth), MVP + customer validation, business model. Pitch panel: faculty + entrepreneurs + investors. Decision in ~1 week post-pitch.
- **Funding/equity**: up to ₹40L seed (Avishkar); equity terms not publicly stated; 53% follow-on funding rate.
- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: DPIIT-recognized; AIC Social Tech Incubator (NITI Aayog AIM); OJAS MedTech (DBT-BIRAC).
- **Sector/stage filter**: AI/ML, CV, NLP, AR/VR, Robotics, DeepTech. Requires registered Indian co + MVP + customer validation. STRONG FIT for ThoughtKraft.
- **Red flags**: highly competitive; "deep tech" framing may favor research-heavy plays.
- **ThoughtKraft fit**: STRONG — explicit AI/ML + customer validation + post-revenue alignment. Lead with AI-infra IP + 4 paying customers (SkinInspired/StayVista/Monarch + Flipkart project). Hyderabad relocation only if accepted.
