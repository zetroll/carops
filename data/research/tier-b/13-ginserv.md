# GINSERV (Global Incubation Services, Bangalore)

- **URL**: https://ginserv.com/
- **Apply**: https://ginserv.com/incubation/ (Open Incubation, ASCENT, GINCELERATOR 3.0)
- **Intake model**: Multiple programs — ASCENT 6-month curated cohort; GINCELERATOR 3.0 6-month accelerator; Open Incubation rolling.
- **Next deadline (60-90d)**: rolling for Open Incubation; ASCENT rolling for early PoC; GINCELERATOR 3.0 cohort dates not surfaced for 2026.
- **Reapply policy**: not surfaced.
- **Selection committee / board (public)**: not surfaced; JSS Mahavidyapeetha governance umbrella; specific names not public.
- **Stated criteria**: stage, MVP/PoC validation, team, scalability, sector fit (especially health/medtech for GINCELERATOR).
- **Funding/equity**: GINCELERATOR 2.0 gave ₹25L toolkit per startup; SISFS pass-through available.
- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: DST-TBI, NIDHI-SSS, SISFS-supported.
- **Sector/stage filter**: GINCELERATOR 3.0 = healthtech/medtech only; ASCENT = idea/early-PoC; Open Incubation broader. B2B SaaS fits Open Incubation, not GINCELERATOR.
- **Red flags**: programs heavily skewed health/medtech in 2025; AI-infra/SaaS may have weak channel here.
- **ThoughtKraft fit**: WEAK-MEDIUM — Open Incubation only. Better as parallel-shotgun than focus. Bangalore physical presence may be required.
