- **Apply**: https://www.iimleic.com/incubation

- **Stated criteria**: team strength, market opportunity, tech/IP, financial viability, alignment with funding objectives; due-diligence after pitch.

- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: DPIIT-recognized; SISFS approved; requires DPIIT-recognized startup, 51% Indian promoter shareholding.
- **Sector/stage filter**: AI, Blockchain, IIoT, Digital Healthcare, Cloud, VR, 3D Printing; MVP/prototype-ready early stage.
- **Red flags**: none surfaced.
