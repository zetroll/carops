# iTNT Hub (Tamil Nadu Technology Hub)

- **URL**: https://itnthub.tn.gov.in/
- **Apply**: stakeholder registration on portal homepage (startup category)
- **Intake model**: Hub/network model — nodal agency for TN Deep Tech Startup Policy 2025-26. Cohort-style challenges + rolling network membership.
- **Next deadline (60-90d)**: TN Deep Tech Startup Policy launched 8 Jan 2026 at UmagineTN. ₹100Cr fund over 5 years targeting 100 deeptech startups. First cohorts being seeded Q2 2026.
- **Reapply policy**: not surfaced.
- **Selection committee / board (public)**:
  - Vanitha Venugopal — CEO, iTNT Hub (ex-Dell Director, Stanford EMBA)
  - Board: industry members + Govt of TN/IT&DS Dept oversight (specific names not surfaced)
- **Stated criteria**: deep tech / emerging tech focus (AI, quantum, robotics); R&D depth, lab-to-market readiness.
- **Funding/equity**: ₹100Cr policy fund; specific per-startup terms TBD per challenge.
- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: MeitY (GoI) + IT&DS (GoTN) backed; not a SISFS incubator itself but works with state ecosystem.
- **Sector/stage filter**: Deep tech / emerging tech. AI infra fits, but priority is hard tech (quantum, semiconductors, robotics). B2B SaaS without deep IP may be borderline.
- **Red flags**: brand new policy (Jan 2026) — process unproven; "deep tech" gating may exclude pure SaaS.
- **ThoughtKraft fit**: MEDIUM — AI-infra positioning works if pitched as deep-tech IP. Tamil Nadu state focus. Worth a stakeholder registration; low cost to wait for first challenges.
