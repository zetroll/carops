# ACIC-KL (KL Startups Foundation, KL University, Vijayawada)

- **URL**: https://www.acickl.in/
- **Apply**: acickl.in/community-innovator-fellowship + aim.gov.in; acic.klstartups@kluniversity.in
- **Intake model**: Cohort + fellowship (Community Innovator Fellowship) + Pre-Incubation/Incubation/Acceleration tracks.
- **Next deadline (60-90d)**: not surfaced; rolling fellowship intake.
- **Reapply policy**: not surfaced.
- **Selection committee / board (public)**:
  - RamanKanth Vallabhaneni — CEO, ACIC-KL Startups Foundation
  - K. Siva Kanchana Latha — Executive Director
  - Dr. G. Pardha Saradhi Varma — VC, KL University; chief mentor
  - Er. Koneru Raja Hareen — VP, KL University
  - Ravi Eswarapu — Advisor, Innovation & Incubation
- **Stated criteria**: community impact within 30km of Vijayawada/Guntur (or external with community-hours mandate), grassroots innovation, scalability.
- **Funding/equity**: Community Innovator Fellowship up to ₹2L; ACIC grant pool (₹2.5 Cr/5 yrs centre level).
- **DPIIT/SISFS/AIM**: AIM/NITI Aayog ACIC; Section 8.
- **Sector/stage filter**: grassroots/community; tier 2/3 + rural; idea → early-stage. Urban B2B SaaS off-archetype unless pitched as MSME/community enablement.
- **Red flags**: 30km geographic bias; community/rural mandate disadvantages urban B2B SaaS.
- **ThoughtKraft fit**: WEAK — geographic and thesis mismatch. Skip unless explicitly framed as MSME upliftment in Andhra/Telangana.
