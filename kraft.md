## Capital Ask Policy

**Grants only.** Equity acceptable ONLY if:
- ≤2–3% dilution AND
- ≥₹20L cheque

This rules out most VC-style accelerators (Upekkha, Axilor, India Accelerator standard terms). Optimises for SISFS pass-through, NIDHI-SSS, AIM/ACIC grants, T-Hub-style no-equity programs, NSRCEL Launchpad, GSF Founders Academy, SINE/IITMIC NIDHI-PRAYAS, NASSCOM (no-equity).

| Tier of incubator | Ask | Equity ceiling | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier A (AIC/ACIC/regional TBI) | ₹10–30L | 0% | SISFS / DPIIT grant |
| Tier B (IIM/IIT/specialist) | ₹25–50L | up to 3% (only if ≥₹20L) | NIDHI-SSS / SISFS / convertible |
| Tier C (CIIE / NSRCEL / SINE / IITMIC) | up to ₹1Cr | up to 3% (only if ≥₹20L) | grant or low-dilution convertible |

**Hard floor:** never accept >3% equity, period. Below the ₹20L threshold, refuse equity entirely.

**LLP form caveat:** ThoughtKraft is an LLP. SISFS, NIDHI-SSS, AIM/ACIC grants accept LLPs. Programs that require equity issuance (Pvt Ltd shares: Upekkha, Axilor, India Accelerator standard) are structurally incompatible without conversion. This *coincides* with the grant-only preference, so it is not a blocker — it just means the grant track is doubly the right path.
