# Mode: ic-prep — Investment Committee pitch prep

When the user has been invited to pitch to a specific incubator's IC and says "prep me for {X} IC", "I'm pitching {X} on {date}", produce a tailored pitch-prep packet.

## Required reads

1. `kraft.md`, `founder.md`, `data/proof-points.md`
2. `reports/incubators/{rank}-{slug}.md` — incubator strategy
3. `reports/selection-committees.md` — IC member roster
4. `data/research/tier-{a,b,c}/{NN-slug}.md` — verified research
5. (optional) WebSearch each public IC member's recent activity, portfolio, public talks — surfaces fresh signal

## Packet contents

### 1. The 60-second pitch

The opener. Three numbers, one positioning sentence, one paying customer named. Practice this until it's automatic — IC starts cold; you have 60 seconds.

Template:
> "ThoughtKraft is the operating system for Indian commerce — one AI-infrastructure layer replacing 8–15 fragmented tools. Today: ₹1L MRR, 3 paying customers including SkinInspired, a Unilever-funded D2C brand. ₹70K → ₹1L over 3 months bootstrapped, 7 modules in production. Built by ex-Amazon Sr. PM (3 years internal Gen AI), IIFT Delhi + MIT Manipal."

### 2. Per-IC-member talk track

For each named IC member (from `selection-committees.md`), draft a 30-second talking point that resonates with their background.

Example for IIMCIP IC:
- Hari Balasubramanian: SaaS metrics → "Our gross margin profile and ARPA suggest a path to ₹X ARR by month Y."
- Sachin Oswal: operator → "Customer-acquisition was founder-led; let me walk you through the pattern."
- Ajay Jain (Chair): strategic → "Where we'd grow with IIMCIP support is in [specific]."

### 3. Anticipated questions + best answers

Build a 10-Q rehearsal list. Common categories:
- Why solo? — "Co-founder hire is the next 90-day priority. The role I want to fill is X."
- What's the moat? — three answers from `proof-points.md` defensibility section.
- Competition? — name 3 (Tally, Vyapar, Khatabook) and explain ThoughtKraft's wedge.
- What if BigSaaS clones? — see kraft.md defensibility.
- Why this incubator (not the other 5)? — specific to `reports/incubators/{slug}.md` "why apply" section.
- Capital ask + use-of-funds? — respect 3% / ₹20L policy; have unit-economics-backed answer.
- Reference customer? — pre-seed which of the 3 paying customers you'd put on a reference call.
- Failure mode? — see `proof-points.md` "Failure stories" — IC will ask.
- Exit thesis? — careful answer; ICs want fundability, founder wants optionality.
- Awkward Q (e.g., father's Reliance role)? — pre-rehearse a calm 30-sec answer.

### 4. The "ask" slide rehearsal

Practice saying the capital ask out loud. Founders fumble this. Hard line: "₹50L grant via SISFS, no equity. If you offer a convertible, our cap policy is ≥₹16Cr to keep dilution ≤3%."

## Output to disk

`reports/ic-prep/{rank}-{slug}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` — the packet plus a rehearsal log.

## Day-of checklist

- Deck open and scrubbed for typos.
- 1-page memo printed (some ICs want hard copy).
- DPIIT cert + LLP MoA on hand for any compliance questions.
- Customer reference numbers + emails staged in case IC asks live.
- Phone on silent.
- 30-min buffer before pitch to re-read this file.
