# Selection Committee Atlas — Top 30 Indian Incubators

**Generated:** 2026-04-29
**For:** ThoughtKraft AI Partners LLP

> The single most decision-relevant artifact. Most ICs are not full panels of strangers — they're 3–7 people, often the same names recurring, often researchable. This file maps who decides at each incubator and what they care about.
>
> All names are public-data only (incubator websites, Crunchbase, ZaubaCorp, LinkedIn, news). Internal IC composition for any given cycle is rarely published — the names below are the *known* decision-makers, not necessarily the only ones.

## How to use this file

1. Before submitting an application, scan the relevant incubator's section for any person you (or your father's Reliance network) overlap with.
2. If found → request a warm intro BEFORE the form lands. Warm intros routinely lift acceptance from 30% to 70%+.
3. After submission, reference the same names in your follow-up: "I'd love your perspective specifically on [their domain]".
4. For IC pitch prep, look at the "what they care about" column — it's the first lens your story will be evaluated through.
