## Application strategy

### 1. Use the alumni network BEFORE submitting

MUTBI's biggest filter is "do we trust this founder". An MIT-Manipal alumni intro converts a cold form into a warm read.

- LinkedIn-search "MIT Manipal alumni MUTBI" → find anyone who's been through MUTBI as a portfolio founder.
- Ask for a 15-min coffee — *before* submission.
- Mention you're applying. Ask: "what does Dr. Santhosha Rao look for?".

### 2. Frame ThoughtKraft as a Karnataka-extensible play

MUTBI cares about Karnataka jobs. ThoughtKraft has Bangalore-area customers (StayVista has Karnataka properties; SkinInspired has KA-based supply chain — verify). Use this:

- "Our customer SkinInspired (Unilever-funded) deploys Kraft across Karnataka and Maharashtra D2C operations."
- "MUTBI's ecosystem unlocks 50+ MAHE-affiliated MSMEs as direct adopters of Kraft."

### 3. Equity terms — tread carefully

MUTBI's "service+equity agreement" is unusual. Read the fine print. It typically:
- Gives services + infra in exchange for ~3-5% equity.
- Stacks with NIDHI grant if you take both.

ThoughtKraft's policy: ≤3%. If MUTBI offers 5%, push back ("we'd take 2.5% for services + waive equity on the grant portion"). Walk away if they insist on >3%.

## Anti-patterns

- Don't lead with "ex-Amazon" if it crowds out "MIT Manipal alum". The alumni gate is literally what gets you in the door — lead with it.
- Don't apply to MUTBI if your LLP form is a blocker for service+equity (LLPs can't issue traditional equity — verify with their team that LLP-form startups are accepted).
- Don't oversell solo-founder. Have a 90-day co-founder hire roadmap ready.
