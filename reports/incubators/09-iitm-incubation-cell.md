# Strategy — IIT Madras Incubation Cell (IITMIC) (rank 9, prob 45%)

**Status:** ⏳ Rolling, periodic IC review.
**Decision body:** IITMIC Board — Chair Prof. V. Kamakoti (Director, IIT Madras); Operational lead Dr. Tamaswati Ghosh (CEO since 2013).
**Capital:** **Equity-free grants** via NIDHI-PRAYAS (~₹10L) + NIDHI-SSS (up to ₹50L) + SISFS pass-through. Equity investment via IITM RuTAG/Pravartak/IPSF for select startups (skip — equity).

## Why this is rank-9 not higher

IITMIC has a 500+ portfolio with 2 unicorns and Ather IPO. Brand is stellar. But:
- **Strong deep-tech bias** — pure SaaS faces "fit" challenges.
- Highly competitive — they can afford to be picky.
- Founder is non-IIT-M (no alumni preference); 55% of portfolio is external founders so it's not blocking, just neutral.

## Application strategy

### 1. Reframe ThoughtKraft as deep-tech AI infrastructure

Same advice as SINE, but more aggressive. IITMIC's deep-tech bar is higher than SINE's.

- ❌ "We make AI tools for MSMEs."
- ✓ "We are building India's commerce-AI infrastructure substrate. The 7 modules implement [novel-technique-1] for vernacular retrieval and [novel-technique-2] for cross-module agent orchestration. Open-source contributions: __FILL__."

If ThoughtKraft has any open-source contributions, public benchmarks, or technical writing — *lead* with those for IITMIC.

### 2. Connect via IITMIC portfolio cos

500+ companies = high probability of overlap. ThoughtKraft customers (Monarch, StayVista, SkinInspired) may have IITM-affiliated leadership. LinkedIn-search "IIT Madras" + customer name.

### 3. NIDHI-PRAYAS is the entry door

Same as SINE: ₹10L prototype grant is fast, low-friction, builds credibility for a follow-on NIDHI-SSS application.

## Anti-patterns

- Don't apply with the "commerce-OS" frame. IITMIC will read it as "yet another vertical SaaS". Apply with the **AI-infra** frame.
- Don't oversell traction without IP. ₹1L MRR is fine, but their ICs want defensibility theses, not just metrics.
