## Application strategy — the 3 things to get exactly right

### 1. Frame it as an AI-INFRASTRUCTURE play, not a vertical SaaS

NASSCOM Emerge 50 is competing for India's "global AI champion" narrative. They want infra plays. So:

- ❌ "Kraft is a workflow automation tool for Indian MSMEs"
- ✓ "Kraft is the AI-infrastructure layer for Indian commerce — replacing 8–15 fragmented tools with one platform. Live in production with Unilever-funded brand SkinInspired, StayVista, Monarch Networth Capital, and a paid Flipkart project."

### 2. Lead with three numbers in the first 30 seconds

- **₹1L MRR** (currency-precise — don't say "₹1+ lakh", say "₹100,000")
- **3 paying customers + 1 enterprise project** (Flipkart)
- **7 modules in 9 months solo + bootstrapped** = execution velocity

### 3. Anchor "why now" to govt rails

NASSCOM panels respond to the IndiaAI Mission narrative. Cite ONDC, GST e-invoicing, UPI, Startup India SISFS — ThoughtKraft sits at the intersection.

## Anti-patterns (do NOT do)

- Do NOT call Unilever a customer. SkinInspired is a Unilever-funded brand. Get this exact phrasing right — NASSCOM jurors fact-check.
- Do NOT pitch as a generic SaaS. There are 5,000 GenAI-features-on-SaaS plays in India; you're not one of them.
- Do NOT lead with the founder's father / Reliance pedigree. NASSCOM cares about *your* operator chops, not nepotism signals.
- Do NOT promise capital — they don't give it. Promise traction milestones that justify their investor connects.
