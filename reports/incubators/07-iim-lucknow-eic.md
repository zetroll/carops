If you only got into one program in the next 6 months, IIM Lucknow EIC's ₹75L combined ticket would be the financial standout (vs. AIC-BIMTECH's ₹70L SISFS pass-through which has a more dilutive convertible structure).

But — it's a polished, B-school review. The application has to be polished to match.

## Convertible math you must do BEFORE accepting

OCD = Optionally Convertible Debenture. CCD = Compulsorily Convertible Debenture. CCPS = Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares.

- OCD/CCD at ₹50L → typically converts at next priced round. Push for cap ≥ ₹15Cr (effective ~3.3% dilution).
- CCPS at ₹25L → preference shares; usually converts 1:1 to equity. Push for cap ≥ ₹8Cr (effective ~3% dilution).
- Combined: ₹75L for ~6%+ dilution if caps are weak. **Above the 3% policy line.**

**ACTION:** Negotiate hard. If combined dilution >5%, take only the ₹50L OCD with a ≥₹15Cr cap. Refuse the ₹25L CCPS or ask for grant conversion.
