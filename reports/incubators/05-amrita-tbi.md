## Application strategy

### 1. Reference Yellow.ai in your application

Yellow.ai is Amrita TBI's most visible SaaS portfolio company. Mentioning a portfolio precedent shows the IC you've done your homework AND you understand what they value.

- "ThoughtKraft sits at the intersection of AI infrastructure and Indian commerce — similar to how Yellow.ai built a conversational-AI infrastructure layer that scaled out of Amrita TBI."

### 2. Lead the pitch with capital efficiency, not vision

Amrita's IC sees a lot of pitches. They reward:
- Bootstrapped → revenue (✓ ThoughtKraft fits perfectly)
- Solo or small team that ships fast (✓)
- 2-3 year deployment plan with milestones (build this)

The narrative arc: "₹0 → ₹70K → ₹1L MRR in 9 months on bootstrapped budget; with ₹50L SISFS + ₹50L NSTEDB we project ₹X MRR by month Y, deploying ₹Z to engineering and ₹W to GTM."

### 3. Use the Snehal Shetty / Sreerag Rajan path

Snehal Shetty (COO) is the operational decision lead for incubation applications. Her LinkedIn is active. A 200-word personalized message before submission moves you from "form #137" to "I read your application".

## Anti-patterns

- Don't overuse "AI" buzzwords. Amrita IC has seen 10,000 AI pitches. Show *deployment*, not buzzword density.
- Don't ask for the full ₹1Cr immediately. Better: "₹50L SISFS now, with planned NSTEDB top-up after we hit milestones at month 12".
- Don't ignore the ≤2yr SISFS rule. If LLP is older, structure the request differently (NSTEDB / TDB, not SISFS).
