# Strategy — IIM Calcutta Innovation Park (IIMCIP) (rank 10, prob 45%)

**Status:** ⏳ Rolling main incubation; cohort programs (Udgama, Bengal Business Accelerator, etc.).
**Decision body:** Investment Committee with **named members** (the most-named IC of any program in this list).
- IC: Hari Balasubramanian, Sachin Oswal, Anuj Sharma, Uday Chatterjee, Sooraj Dhawan, Ajay Jain (Chair).
- Board: Dr. V.K. Rai (CEO), Gaurav Kapoor (CBO), Kanchan Jain (Ascertis Credit), Prof. Alok Rai (IIM-C), Prof. Bhaskar Chakrabarti.

**Capital:** SISFS pass-through up to ₹50L; **NIDHI-SSS up to ₹1Cr equity-light**. The ₹1Cr ceiling is one of the highest on the list.

## Why this is uniquely actionable

The IC roster is *publicly named*. Most other Top-30 programs have a Black Box IC. IIMCIP's transparency means:
- Each IC member is researchable on LinkedIn.
- Each IC member can be sent a tailored 200-word thesis pre-application.
- The conversation continues *into* the IC meeting itself, not just from the form.

This is rare and you should exploit it.

## Application strategy

### 1. Pre-engagement campaign — 6 emails over 2 weeks

Send each IC member a tailored note before the application:
- **Hari Balasubramanian** (typically a SaaS/tech generalist) — lead with the AI-infra thesis.
- **Sachin Oswal** (industry/operator background — verify on LinkedIn) — lead with the customer logos.
- **Anuj Sharma** — lead with execution velocity (7 modules in 9 months solo bootstrapped).
- **Uday Chatterjee, Sooraj Dhawan** — verify backgrounds on LinkedIn first; tailor.
- **Ajay Jain (Chair)** — most strategic. Send last, after the others have replied.

Don't ask for a meeting. Just open a thesis-fit conversation. Then: "We're applying to IIMCIP next month — would value 15 min of your perspective."

### 2. Apply to the main pan-India track, not Bengal Business

Bengal Business Accelerator restricts to West Bengal-incorporated entities. ThoughtKraft is not WB-incorporated. Skip that track.

### 3. Pitch the NIDHI-SSS ₹1Cr separately

Once accepted into main incubation, ask explicitly about the NIDHI-SSS path. ICs get to pick from a slate; if you've shown traction during incubation, the ₹1Cr ticket follows.

## Action checklist

- [ ] Submit via iimcip.org/incubation/apply.
- [ ] Send 6 IC-member LinkedIn messages over 2 weeks.
- [ ] Build a 1-page memo specifically referencing 1-2 NIDHI-SSS portfolio cos that resemble ThoughtKraft.
- [ ] If invited to pitch, prepare a deeper financial model (B-school standard).

## Reapply

- ~6 months for main track. Required delta: a 50%+ revenue jump or a new tier-1 logo. ThoughtKraft's natural growth meets this in ~6 months.
