### 2. Frame it as commerce-OS, not generic AI

BIMTECH is a B-school — they read "commerce" fluently. Lean into it:

- ❌ "We build AI agents for various business workflows."
- ✓ "Operating system for Indian commerce. Replaces 8–15 fragmented tools. Live with 3 paying customers including a Unilever-funded brand."

### 3. Show capital efficiency obsessively

121 startups onboarded 2025; ₹6.7Cr disbursed. That's an **average ticket of ~₹5.5L** per startup — they spread thin. To get the higher end of the SISFS ticket (₹20L grant + ₹50L convertible), you must demonstrate clear capital-efficient deployment.

- Pre-build the unit economics: "₹20L would buy us 12 months of ENG-3 hire + GTM kit; ₹50L convertible would extend runway 18 months at current burn".

## Anti-patterns

- Don't apply to Khoj just because it's easier — Gurukul track has the SISFS pass-through.
- Don't oversell solo-founder. AIC-BIMTECH IIAC sees a lot of founders; they'll give a fair read but they like to see a co-founder hire roadmap. Show that you know the gap and have a plan.
- Don't promise 100x returns in 3 yrs. They want realistic, defensible numbers. SaaS with 13% MoM should reach ₹X by month Y — math, not vibes.

## Convertible debenture mechanics — the "2-3% nominal" line

Founder's policy: equity OK only if ≤3% AND ≥₹20L. AIC-BIMTECH's SISFS convertible at ₹50L COULD trigger this. Need to read the term sheet carefully:

- Convertible debentures from SISFS typically convert at the next priced round (often with a discount + cap).
- Worst case: a ₹50L convertible at a ₹5Cr cap = effective 10% — *too dilutive*.
- Best case: a ₹50L convertible at a ₹15Cr cap = effective ~3.3% — borderline acceptable.

**ACTION: when offered, push for cap ≥ ₹16Cr or refuse the convertible portion (take only the ₹20L grant).** Treat the ₹20L as a clean grant.
