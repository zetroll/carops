## Application strategy

### 1. Reframe ThoughtKraft as MSME-uplift (don't lie, but *frame*)

The ACIC mandate is "community innovation". Pure urban B2B SaaS is off-thesis. But ThoughtKraft *is* an MSME-enablement play — just describe it that way:

- ❌ "We sell AI tools to enterprises and D2C brands."
- ✓ "We make enterprise-grade AI infrastructure affordable enough for India's MSMEs. Today: 3 paying mid-market customers (a step up from typical MSMEs). Tomorrow: ₹10K/month price points for Tier-2/3 retail."

### 2. Use the IAS-board-member language (not Silicon Valley language)

Dr. Lalit K. Panwar IAS sits on the VGU Board. IAS officers respond to specific patterns:
- "Jobs created" — pre-compute this. ThoughtKraft can claim X jobs across customer ops teams thanks to Kraft.
- "Rural/Tier-2/3 footprint" — cite the geographic distribution of customers if any are non-metro.
- "Govt scheme alignment" — DPIIT ✓, SISFS-ready, MSME-Sahayak adjacency.

### 3. Lower the deck production value but raise the substance

This is not a Silicon Valley pitch. A clean Google-Slides deck with substantive numbers > a polished Figma deck with vague vision slides.

## Anti-patterns

- Don't drop big logos as your headline. SkinInspired/StayVista/Monarch are *enterprise* logos — fine to mention but the IC will pattern-match "this guy doesn't really serve MSMEs". Lead with the affordability narrative; back it with logos as proof.
- Don't ask for the full ₹50L unless you can show MSME-specific deployment math.
- Don't mention competitive global SaaS players (HubSpot, Salesforce) — irrelevant to this audience. Mention Tally, Vyapar, Khatabook instead.
