# Strategy — NSRCEL (rank 6, prob 60%)

**Status:** 📅 Cohort-based. Watch for next Revenue-Stage Entrepreneurship Program (RSEP) opening, likely Q3 2026.
**Decision body:** Anand Sri Ganesh (CEO), Prof. Srivardhini K Jha (Chairperson) + 50+ mentor panel.
**Capital:** Launchpad ₹5L grant top-3 (no equity). RSEP and other tracks may channel SISFS up to ₹70L.

## Why this is rank-6 not rank-1

NSRCEL has the brand, the alumni network, the IIM-B mentor pool. But it's *competitive* and *cohort-based*: you can't apply tomorrow. So while it's high-value, it's not high-probability *this month*.

## The right track for ThoughtKraft is RSEP

Five tracks confuse most applicants:
- ❌ Launchpad — pre-revenue/early-MVP. Wrong stage.
- ✓ **RSEP (Revenue-Stage Entrepreneurship Program)** — exact fit.
- ❌ Healthcare / Mobility / Women / Goldman — off-thesis.

If a Launchpad cycle opens before RSEP and you're impatient, you can technically apply with the post-revenue traction. But your application reads "wrong track" — IC will route or reject. Wait for RSEP.

## Application strategy (when the window opens)

### 1. Engage the IIMB mentor pool BEFORE the form

NSRCEL has a 120+ mentor pool of IIMB alumni and entrepreneurs. Find 2-3 who back B2B SaaS or AI plays. Get a 30-min call to road-test your pitch. Their feedback often surfaces internally — you arrive at the form pre-vetted.

### 2. Cite Prof. Jha's framing

Prof. Srivardhini K Jha publishes on Indian entrepreneurship. Read 2 of her papers (~30 min); cite one in your "vision" slide. Signals seriousness.

### 3. Make your "post-revenue" math airtight

RSEP gates on "founders with traction". Your ₹70K → ₹1L (~13% MoM) is fine but not exceptional. Reach ₹1.5L MRR before applying to elevate to "exceptional".
