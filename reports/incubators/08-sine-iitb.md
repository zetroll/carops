## Application strategy

### 1. Pitch the deep-tech IP angle

SINE's bias is deep-tech. ThoughtKraft is more applied-AI than research-AI. Bridge this:

- "Each of our 7 modules is an applied-research artifact: agentic orchestration, retrieval over commerce graphs, vernacular intent recognition, etc. We are building defensible IP in the AI-orchestration layer for Indian commerce."

If you can name one or two specific technical innovations (proprietary algorithm, novel retrieval scheme, etc.), this is the place to surface them.

### 2. Apply to NIDHI-PRAYAS first, then SISFS

PRAYAS is fast (₹10L in weeks). SISFS is slower (45-day SC + 8-week IC). Stage them.

### 3. The leadership transition is an opportunity

When CEOs change, ICs are recalibrating their book. New CEO ≠ stale playbook. A clean, well-positioned application has an edge while the org is ironing out priorities. Apply soon, while flux helps you.
