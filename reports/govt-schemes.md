# Indian Government Startup Schemes — Channels Map

**Generated:** 2026-04-29
**For:** ThoughtKraft AI Partners LLP

> Most government startup capital flows through a small number of national schemes. Each incubator is a *channel* for one or more of these schemes. Understanding the schemes (not just the incubators) gives you the underlying eligibility, ticket size, and reapply rules.
>
> **The single most-leveraged action for ThoughtKraft:** verify DPIIT recognition is current (it is) and that SISFS eligibility is met → then apply to multiple SISFS-channel incubators in parallel. Same dossier, parallelizable distribution.

## The 5 schemes that matter for ThoughtKraft

| Scheme | Operator | Format | Ticket | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SISFS** (Startup India Seed Fund Scheme) | DPIIT, GoI | Grant + convertible | ₹20L grant (PoC) + ₹50L convertible (commercialization) | DPIIT-recognized, ≤2 yrs incorporation, ≥51% Indian promoter equity, ≤₹10L prior govt funding |
| **NIDHI-SSS** (Seed Support Scheme) | DST NSTEDB | Grant or grant+equity | up to ₹1Cr per startup | TBI-channelled; Section 8 host requirements |
| **NIDHI-PRAYAS** | DST NSTEDB | Pure prototype grant | up to ₹10L | Pre-revenue prototype; some allow up to early-revenue |
| **BIRAC schemes** (BIG, SBIRI) | BIRAC | Grant | up to ₹50L (BIG) | Biotech / health-adjacent only — **not relevant for ThoughtKraft** |
| **MeitY TIDE 2.0** | MeitY | Grant + infrastructure | up to ₹5L stipend + ₹4L grant | AI/IoT/blockchain etc. via 51 TIDE Centres including IIT-D |

## Two more for awareness

- **AIM ACIC** (Atal Innovation Mission Community Innovation Centres) — centre-level grant up to ₹2.5 Cr / 5 yrs. Per-startup ticket comes from SISFS/NIDHI/state schemes channelled by the ACIC.
- **AIM AIC** (Atal Innovation Mission Atal Incubation Centres) — similar, but more established / urban (vs. ACIC's rural tilt).

## Scheme-first strategy for ThoughtKraft

Instead of thinking "which incubator do I apply to?", think "which **scheme** is my best fit, then which incubators channel it?".

### Scheme A: SISFS (highest-priority for ThoughtKraft)

- **Eligibility check:**
  - DPIIT-recognized ✓
  - ≤2 yrs incorporation: **VERIFY** ThoughtKraft AI Partners LLP date.
  - ≥51% Indian promoter equity ✓ (founder + father bootstrap).
  - ≤₹10L prior govt funding ✓ (none).
  - LLP form: **eligible** for SISFS (per startupindia.gov.in FAQ).
- **Strategy:** ONE polished SISFS dossier (deck + memo + financials + DPIIT cert). Submit to multiple SISFS-channel incubators in parallel: AIC-BIMTECH, AIC-VGU, KIIT-TBI, Amrita TBI, NSRCEL, IIM Lucknow EIC, FITT-IITD, IIMCIP, SINE, IITMIC.
- **Reapply rule:** 3 months after rejection email; no edit-and-resubmit on rejected applications.
- **Each incubator's IIAC (Incubator IC) decides separately** — same dossier, ~10x distribution, 10x chance.

### Scheme B: NIDHI-SSS (₹1Cr cap — biggest single ticket)

- **Channels:** IIMCIP (highest-cap), SINE, IITMIC, DERBI Start-to-Scale, GINSERV.
- **Strategy:** different framing than SISFS — "scaling" rather than "seed". Pitch traction (₹70K → ₹1L MRR), unit economics, 6-month deployment plan.

### Scheme C: NIDHI-PRAYAS (₹10L prototype grant)

- **Channels:** SINE, IITMIC.
- **Lower priority for ThoughtKraft** — already past prototype stage with 7 modules in production. But still a low-effort apply if rolling.

### Scheme D: MeitY TIDE 2.0 (AI/IoT/Blockchain)

- **Channels:** FITT-IITD is the most relevant. iTNT Hub may channel this in TN cohorts.
- **Strategy:** AI-infra / GenAI angle, MeitY Startup Hub registration, demonstrate "applied AI" use case.

### Scheme E: State scheme (often overlooked)

- **iStart Rajasthan** — founder is Udaipur-based. Investigate state-level grants/recognition that complement national schemes. Often has fast-track for state-resident founders.
- **Maharashtra MSInS** (Bhau channel), **Telangana TASK** (T-Hub), **Karnataka KSUM** (DERBI/MUTBI/GINSERV), **TN Deep Tech ₹100Cr** (iTNT) — state schemes are smaller but easier given low competition.

## Action items for the founder

1. **Verify SISFS eligibility today** — check ThoughtKraft AI Partners LLP incorporation date. If >2 years, the SISFS strategy collapses; pivot to NIDHI-SSS focus.
2. **DPIIT certificate ready** — keep a PDF copy in `data/dpiit-certificate.pdf` (gitignored).
